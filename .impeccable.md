## Design Context

### Users
All ages interested in dance and music classes, as well as studio room hire. They are creative individuals, instructors, and students looking for a seamless way to discover and book artistic spaces and learning opportunities.

### Brand Personality
Creative, elegant, artistic. The voice should feel inspiring and refined, moving away from corporate rigidity toward a more expressive, "muse-like" experience.

### Aesthetic Direction
Colorful yet sophisticated, utilizing smooth lines and fluid compositions. The interface should feel like a canvas for the arts, avoiding the blocky, generic "SaaS" look. Think high-quality editorial meets a modern art gallery.

### Design Principles
1. **Artistic Fluidity**: Use smooth, organic lines and transitions. Avoid sharp, aggressive corners and rigid, monotonous grids.
2. **Elegant Color**: Use color purposefully to inspire and differentiate, but maintain elegance through sophisticated palettes rather than neon "tech" vibes.
3. **Gallery Presentation**: Treat courses and rooms like exhibits in a gallery—generous whitespace, high-quality imagery, and clear, beautiful typography.
4. **Non-Corporate Warmth**: Avoid the generic "enterprise" look (cold grays, standard buttons, hero metrics). Use warmth, texture, and refined details to create a premium, artistic feel.
